# Glossary: Heat Transfer Terms

- **Advection:** bulk transport of thermal energy by a moving fluid from one place to another. Energy flow rate: ṁ·c_p·ΔT (W).
- **Heat flux (q″):** rate of heat transfer per unit area, in W/m² (or W/cm²). q″ = Q̇ / A.
- **Thermal conductivity (k):** material property measuring how easily heat conducts through a substance, in W/(m·K).
- **Convective heat transfer coefficient (h):** factor relating convective heat flux to the temperature difference between a surface and a fluid, q″ = h·(T_s − T_∞), in W/(m²·K).
- **Thermal equilibrium:** state where objects and their surroundings reach the same temperature, so net heat flow stops.
- **Heat sink:** component, often a finned metal piece, that absorbs and dissipates heat away from a device.
